# Report Writing

Give the reader the conclusion, its evidence, and the next decision in the
fewest well-structured words, while preserving numbers, sources, and claim
strength.

## Load references conditionally

- Read [report-style.md](references/report-style.md) before drafting or
  restructuring any report.

## Core contract

1. Preserve numbers, denominators, sources, labels, terminology, qualifiers,
   and claim strength.
2. Trace every new or strengthened factual or causal statement to the
   supplied evidence. Report evidence gaps instead of filling them with
   plausible text.
3. Lead with the answer: state the finding or recommendation first, then the
   supporting structure, then the detail the reader may skip.
4. Respect the requested mode:
   - review-only returns findings without editing files;
   - language-only preserves structure, density, and unaffected wording;
   - structural revision reorganizes only the authorized scope.
5. Keep one term per concept across the whole document; introduce
   abbreviations once and reuse them.

## Workflow

1. Establish audience, decision context, and the report's job: inform,
   recommend, or record.
2. Record protected facts, sources, and the applicable edit contract.
3. Choose the structure from report-style.md that matches the report type;
   adapt sections to the material instead of forcing a template.
4. Draft top-down: conclusion, then sections, then paragraphs; keep each
   section to one message.
5. Recheck claims against sources, verify totals and cross-references, and
   compare the final diff with the edit contract.

## Output

Deliver the requested document or review findings. Report protected evidence,
checks actually run, unavailable validation, and any unresolved source or
scope conflict.
